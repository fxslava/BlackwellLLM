| 16-entry LUT (Lloyd-Max, NF4) | 1 | 16 | 8 | 7.0 | 13.1 us | 26.2% |
| E8 32-bit coset pack | 1 | 25 | 0 | 5.1 | 9.6 us | 19.2% |

One tensor is 28.1 MB at 4.013 bpw; reading it at 564 GB/s takes 50 us. all formats read identical bytes, so at batch=1 the GEMV is bandwidth-bound and the dequant ALU is hidden; the LUT variants' risk is not op count but shared-memory bank conflicts.

Notes per format:

- **RTN INT4 / any 4-bit uniform** -- 8x (shift+mask), 8x bias subtract; no table
- **16-entry LUT (Lloyd-Max, NF4)** -- 8 indexed reads; shared-memory bank conflicts unless the table is register-resident via a select tree (~4 selects/weight) or PRMT
- **E8 32-bit coset pack** -- 7x (shift+mask), 1x high-3 extract, parity via __popc(word & mask), coset half-step folded into the dequant bias; pure ALU, no table, no divergence

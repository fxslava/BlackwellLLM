Where answers come from

Off, the local model only transcribes what you said and the reply comes from the remote assistant. On, the same local model also writes the answer. Either way the reply is gated by the same rule — a turn that was interrupted or cut off is never answered at all. Takes effect on your next sentence.

Which microphone is listened to. With system audio capture switched on below, this list becomes the speakers instead — that mode records what an output device is playing, so it is an output device it has to be pointed at.

Applied before the voice detector and the transcriber, so the bar shows exactly what they are given. Speak normally: peaks that never leave the left third mean the detector will struggle to notice you.

The microphone stays open while the assistant speaks — nothing is muted — and the assistant's own voice is subtracted from what it hears. That is what lets you interrupt by simply talking over it, on open speakers, without it interrupting itself. Turning this off is a diagnostic, not a mode: the microphone then carries the loudspeaker unchanged, and unless you are wearing headphones the assistant will cut off its own answers.

Adapts fasterBigger room

How much of the speaker→room→microphone path the canceller can model. It has to cover the whole delay plus the reverberation tail; echo arriving later than this cannot be cancelled at all. Raise it in a large or echoey room — longer also means slower to settle after playback starts, so bigger is not safer.

Speculative decoding

While you are still talking, audio captured so far is prefilled into the model ahead of time, so less work is left when you stop. It cannot change what is heard — only how much of the answer has already been paid for by the time your sentence ends.

Assistant persona

Who the assistant is, and how it should answer. It is prefilled into the model once and that cache is reused by every utterance afterwards — which is what makes answers start quickly. Changing it therefore rebuilds the cache, so applying takes a moment on a real checkpoint, and clears the conversation the model remembers.

This persona applies to answering only. Hearing you is a separate context with its own instruction below, so the assistant's personality can never talk the transcriber into replying to your speech instead of writing it down.

Speech task

What to do with each thing you say. The task below is free to change — it lives in the per-turn instruction rather than a cached prefix, so it applies on your very next sentence with no rebuild.

The language you speak. Leave it empty and the model works it out from the sound — which it gets wrong on short phrases between close languages, answering Russian speech in Polish words or Latin letters. Naming the language here removes the guess: it is written into the listening context and repeated on every utterance.

This is the source language only. It does not constrain a translation — with Translate selected above you still get the target language out.

The persona of the context that listens — kept apart from the assistant persona above so the two cannot bleed into each other. Leave it empty for the built-in transcriber. The language rules and the output tags are added automatically; you only supply the instruction itself.

Changing it rebuilds that context's cache, but it keeps no history, so it applies almost instantly and leaves your conversation alone.
